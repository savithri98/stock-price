About the Platform

StockVision AI: Academic‑Grade Forecasting

StockVision AI is crafted as an academic‑grade product experience, not just a single notebook or script. It connects a deep‑learning model to a clean, multi‑page interface that anyone can understand in minutes.

The system fetches data from Yahoo Finance, prepares it for the LSTM model, generates future price curves and renders them as interactive charts so you can talk about trend, risk and uncertainty with confidence.
